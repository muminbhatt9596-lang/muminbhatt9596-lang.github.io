Business challenge

Engagement does not automatically become business growth. The task is to connect content performance with website traffic, enquiries and qualified leads.

Strategic approach

A funnel framework: improve content relevance, test formats, messages and calls to action, strengthen the step from social to landing page, and judge success by cost per lead, not engagement alone.

Business implication

Improving several funnel stages could lift lead volume and cut cost per lead without raising spend. Engagement is an intermediate signal; qualified leads are the outcome.

How I evaluate performance

The calculations behind the figures on this page.

CTR

Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100

CPC

Ad spend ÷ Clicks

CPL

Ad spend ÷ Leads

CPA

Ad spend ÷ Acquisitions

ROAS

Attributed revenue ÷ Ad spend

Conversion rate

Conversions ÷ Relevant visitors × 100

Engagement rate

Engagements ÷ Reach × 100

Revenue growth

(New revenue − Old revenue) ÷ Old revenue × 100

ROAS is not profit

Product costs, operating expenses and margins still matter.

Leads are not customers

Lead quality and sales conversion decide the real value.

Forecasts are not achievements
